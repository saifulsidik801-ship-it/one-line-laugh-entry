One funny line for make.fun's "The One-Line Laugh" bounty — a single sentence of at most 200 characters, written to `/output/entry.txt` for the house judge to read two at a time and keep the bigger laugh.

The line:

> My bank wished me a happy birthday, then charged me a monthly fee to celebrate.

A birthday wish is warm and free; a monthly fee is neither. The celebration itself is what you get charged for — a real surprise at the end that still makes sense, an everyday truth anyone with a bank account recognises, and nothing wasted.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `./output/entry.txt` when `/output` is not writable, e.g. a local run), after checking the gate itself: one sentence, 1–200 characters, UTF-8.
